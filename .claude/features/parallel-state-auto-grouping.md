# Feature: Automatic "Parallel State" Grouping of Multiple Initial-State Work Trees

## Purpose

Give multiple, mutually-disconnected Initial-State work trees (see `initial-state-groups.md`) real SCXML `<parallel>` semantics instead of leaving them as merely-adjacent siblings — live, on the actual document, not as a display-only or export-only transform. Originated from the same product requirement as `initial-state-groups.md` (`docs/parallel-states-requirement.md`), specifically its "Support for N-Parallel Machines" phase; reintroduces `<parallel>`/region structure previously implemented and reverted (commit `801145d`, reverted in `bf0ab49`) but scoped narrowly to this automatic-grouping trigger, not a general manual "convert to parallel" feature. Current shape: decision `scxml.md` #12.

## User behavior

- The moment a compound `<state>` has **2 or more** distinct Initial-marked work trees among its direct children, and every child belongs to one of them, the editor silently turns that state **into** a `<parallel>` — same id, transitions, onentry/onexit and `viz:` attributes; only the tag changes and its `initial` is dropped. Each work tree goes into its own region `<state id="{initialId}_region" initial="{initialId}">`. A hand-authored `<parallel>` child takes part like any other child.
- Children that aren't part of any work tree (**loose**) never get a region of their own (`scxml.md` #13): the state stays a `<state>`, its regions go into an inserted, invisible `<parallel id="{id}__parallel">`, and the loose children stay beside it, not Initial. **Connecting** a loose state to a state in a region (either direction) moves it into that region; once nothing is loose, the state becomes the `<parallel>` itself again. A state with `<final>` children is **not** converted, since `<parallel>` can't hold `<final>`; the validator reports it instead.
- At the root: `<scxml>` can't itself be a `<parallel>`, so 2+ root-level work trees go into one inserted `<parallel id="__root_parallel">`. Loose root siblings stay beside it and are absorbed into a region the same way when connected. A newly marked work tree joins it as another region.
- Any `<parallel>` with **fewer than 2 regions** turns back into a `<state>` — whether the editor converted it or the user wrote it by hand — and its sole region (if any) becomes its `initial`. Regions are kept as they are, not unwrapped. `__root_parallel` is removed instead, its remaining region moving back to the root.
- In the diagram, a parallel state looks exactly like a compound state (purple dashed border, same fill) and is drilled into the same way. Inside it, **regions are drawn as columns, not nodes**: each region's contents show directly, with no region name, separated by full-height dashed vertical **divider lines** (violet, `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.5)`). An empty region, or a region that is itself a `<parallel>`, is shown as a node. `__root_parallel` is invisible; its regions' contents show at root level.
- Regions are always active, so a region has **no Initial checkbox, badge or toggle**; toggling one is refused with an explanation. States inside a region keep their own Initial badge/toggle (it edits the region's `initial`).
- The whole group can be **dragged as one unit** by grabbing any empty space within its bounding area (cursor: grab).
- Connecting two states that would **cross from one region into a sibling region** is blocked live while dragging a new connection, with the same dismissible-banner UX as the Initial-State-group conflict check.
- "Add State" while viewing inside a `<parallel>` adds a **loose** state, not a region (`addLooseStateToParallel`): the `<parallel>` becomes a `<state>` holding its regions in `{id}__parallel`, with the new state beside it. Copying, cutting, pasting and drag-to-nest keep a `<parallel>` a `<parallel>`; copied regions are carried over as Initial, so pasting 2+ regions into an empty state makes that state a `<parallel>`.

## Internal architecture

- **No marker attributes.** Nothing in the document distinguishes a converted `<parallel>` from a hand-written one; the only special elements are the **transparent** parallels the editor inserts, recognized by reserved ids — `__root_parallel` (`isRootParallel`, `^__root_parallel(_\d+)?$`) directly under `<scxml>`, and `{id}__parallel` (`isInnerParallel`, `__parallel(_\d+)?$`) directly under a compound `<state>`. `isTransparentParallel(el, kind)` in `src/lib/utils/parallel-structure.ts` applies each pattern only for its container kind (`ContainerKind` = `'root' | 'state' | 'parallel' | 'opaque'`), so a `<parallel>` elsewhere that happens to share the id is ordinary. `'opaque'` is for Initial-group analysis: it counts the transparent parallel as one child.
- **Shared structure helpers** (`parallel-structure.ts`, split out so `initial-group-utils.ts` can use them without a circular import): `getChildEntries(container, kind)` — child states with their tag (`'state' | 'parallel'`), seeing through `__root_parallel` at the root; `getLogicalChildStates`; `getRegionDisplayEntries` / `getDisplayChildEntries(container, kind)` — what the diagram shows (regions replaced by their contents).
- **Core algorithm** — `src/lib/utils/parallel-group-normalization.ts`, `normalizeParallelGroups(scxmlDoc)`, recomputing everything fresh on every call:
  1. `migrateLegacyStructure` — strips `viz:auto-parallel` / `viz:auto-region` / `viz:auto-converted` left by older versions, and collapses an old `<state id="X" initial="P"><parallel id="P" viz:auto-parallel="true">…` so X itself is the `<parallel>`.
  2. `collectAllIds` seeds the document-wide id set (`<state>`, `<parallel>`, `<final>`, `<history>`, any depth).
  3. `normalizeChildren` walks bottom-up; for each `<state>` child, `normalizeHost` (below), and for each `<parallel>` child `revertParallelIfNeeded` (< 2 regions → `<state>`), then re-files any child whose tag changed under `.state` / `.parallel` (`assignEntries`).
  4. `normalizeHost(scxml, 'root')` — the same routine for the root, which never becomes a `<parallel>`.
  - `normalizeHost(host, kind)`: groups the host's children outside its transparent parallel into work trees; `absorbLooseIntoRegions` moves loose components connected to exactly one region into it; then either the `<state>` host becomes the `<parallel>` (nothing loose, no `<final>`), the regions go into / stay in the transparent parallel with loose children beside it, or the transparent parallel is unwrapped (< 2 regions).
  - `claimId` gives synthesized ids (`{initialId}_region`, `__root_parallel`) the smallest free `_2`, `_3`… suffix on a clash. Change detection is explicit (`ctx.changed`), so an already-normalized document reports no change and isn't re-serialized.
  - `collectParallelGroups(scxmlDoc)` — read-only query for the diagram: every `<parallel>` with 2+ regions (`ParallelGroupInfo`): `containerId` (the `<parallel>`'s own id, or for a transparent parallel its host's id — null at the root), `parallelId` (wrapper-node key: `{id}__parallel_group`, or a transparent parallel's own id), and each region's column members.
  - `hasAnyChildren(container)` — counts `<parallel>` children too; guards "auto-mark the first child Initial" so it only fires for a genuinely empty container.
- **Initial-group analysis** (`src/lib/utils/initial-group-utils.ts`): `findParentEntry` returns the logical container and its kind; `getInitialIds(container, kind)` treats every child of a `<parallel>` as Initial and expands `__root_parallel` in the root's `initial` to its regions; `isParallelRegion` identifies regions. `groupAnalysisKind` makes group analysis at the root treat `__root_parallel` as **one** child, so its regions are never read as Initial groups; `wouldMergeDistinctGroups` defers whenever an endpoint is a region, and `wouldConflictIfMarkedInitial` / `ToggleInitialStateCommand` refuse regions.
- **Single choke point**: `normalizeContent()` in `src/stores/editor-store.ts` calls `normalizeParallelGroups` from both `setContent` and `setFileInfo` — every mutation path (Commands, diagram edits, file load) is normalized there. The Monaco typing path (`src/app/page.tsx`'s `handleContentChange`) deliberately does **not** normalize on every keystroke — it calls `setContent(newContent, { immediate: false })` for live preview/validation, then commits via `setContent(..., { immediate: true })` once typing pauses, on the same 500ms debounce boundary `trackTextEdit` uses for history entries.
- **Diagram registry**: `registerAllStates` (`src/lib/converters/converter-modules/state-registry.ts`) takes the container kind and registers `getDisplayChildEntries` — so a `<parallel>` registers as a drillable `elementType: 'parallel'` node whose children are its regions' contents, and a region itself gets no registry entry (unless empty or itself a `<parallel>`). `scxml-to-xstate.ts` maps `elementType: 'parallel'` to `stateType: 'parallel'`; `src/lib/utils/visual-style-utils.ts` styles it the same as compound. A column member's Initial badge comes from its region's `initial` (`isInitialInRegionOf`, `layout-positioning.ts`).
- **Layout pipeline order** (`src/lib/converters/scxml-to-xstate.ts`, inside `convertToReactFlow`), in this sequence, each depending on the one before:
  1. `applyDefaultELKLayout` positions every node normally (flat per-level layout, `viz:xywh` priority — see `decisions/visual-diagram.md` #4/#5).
  2. `separateParallelRegions` (`converter-modules/layout-positioning.ts`) pulls each group's columns into non-overlapping horizontal bands — ELK's flat per-level layout has no notion of "region." Runs **immediately** after ELK layout and before anything reads/persists positions (edge-handle selection, `viz:xywh` write-back, wrapper-node synthesis); the math is `computeRegionSeparationTranslations` in `src/lib/layout/parallel-group-bbox.ts`.
  3. `markParallelGroupMembers` flags every column member's node data with `isParallelGroupMember: true`, read back by `resolveEnhancedNodePosition()` (`src/lib/utils/resolve-enhanced-node-position.ts`): normally saved `viz:xywh` wins over the converter's position, but for a group member that would undo step 2 — a real regression ("Parallel State regions stopped moving together with their divider line") before the flag existed.
  4. `computeParallelGroupWrapperNodes` synthesizes one invisible wrapper `ReactFlow` node per group (parent = the group's `containerId`), sized from the union bounding box of its members (`computeParallelGroupBBoxes`). Its `data` carries `memberIds` (for group drag), `regions` (for live divider recomputation) and `dividerLines` (a parse-time snapshot — see Things that must NOT be changed). Inserted **first** via `unshift` so, with every node at `zIndex: 0`, it paints below every member and a click on a member still reaches the member.
- **Wrapper node** (`src/components/diagram/nodes/parallel-group-wrapper-node.tsx`, ReactFlow type `scxmlParallelGroupWrapper`) renders **no visible content** — no border, background or label. Its whole area carries the `.parallel-group-drag-handle` class (the node's `dragHandle`).
- **Group drag**: there's no ReactFlow parent-child relationship between the wrapper and its members, so `expandWrapperPositionChanges` (`src/lib/layout/parallel-group-drag.ts`) expands one position change on the wrapper into one per member, and `visual-diagram.tsx`'s `onNodesChange` moves (and on drag-stop persists) them all. `handleNodeDragStart` tracks the member ids, not the wrapper, for drop-target/reparent detection.
- **Region divider overlay** (`src/components/diagram/parallel/parallel-region-divider-overlay.tsx`) is a viewport-synced sibling to `<ReactFlow>`: each line spans the whole visible canvas height; only its x moves/scales with pan/zoom (`useViewport()` + `computeDividerScreenX`); `pointer-events: none`. `visual-diagram.tsx` pools `parallelDividerXs` across every visible wrapper node. Divider x-positions are **recomputed live** every render from the current `nodes` (`computeLiveParallelDividerXs`), not read off `data.dividerLines`.
- **Moving/copying keeps the tag** (`src/lib/utils/scxml-manipulation-utils.ts`): `findElementById` / `detachElementFromParent` return `{ element, tag }`, `addStateToDocument` takes the tag, the state clipboard records `copiedParallelIds` (`src/stores/state-clipboard-store.ts`), and detach / `isDescendantOf` / `cloneStateSubtreeWithFreshIds` / `rewriteOrDropTransitions` walk `.parallel` as well as `.state`. A `<parallel>` drop or paste target never gets an `initial`.

## SCXML behavior

- The structure is plain, standards-conformant SCXML `<parallel>` usage with no editor-specific attributes beyond the usual `viz:` layout metadata. A file written by hand with a `<parallel>` is treated exactly like one the editor produced.
- `wouldCrossParallelRegions` enforces W3C SCXML §3.4's rule that no transition may cross from one region of a `<parallel>` into a sibling region (LCCA-derived — see `git show 801145d:docs/parallel-states-scxml-spec.md` §1.4). It walks the live ancestor chain, so it applies to every `<parallel>`.

## Validation rules

- **Live, at connect-time** — `wouldCrossParallelRegions` is checked in both `onConnect` and `isValidConnection` in `visual-diagram.tsx` (the same dual-enforcement pattern `initial-state-groups.md` uses for group-merge conflicts).
- **Static** — `initial-group-validator.ts` skips group analysis inside a `<parallel>` (its children are regions), analyzes the root with `__root_parallel` as one child, and reports a compound state with 2+ Initial States that can't be converted because it has `<final>` children.
- **No static validator for cross-region transitions** — see Known limitations.
- **No nested parallels** (`decisions/scxml.md` #14) — `parallel-nesting-rules.ts` blocks any edit that, once normalized, would put a `<parallel>` inside another one: marking a second Initial inside a region (`ToggleInitialStateCommand`; the checkbox stays enabled and the refusal shows in the canvas warning banner), connecting a loose state that holds a parallel into a region (`onConnect`/`isValidConnection`), paste, and drag-to-nest. `parallel-nesting-validator.ts` reports nesting in hand-written XML.

## Known limitations

- **No static validator for cross-region transitions.** A hand-edited or pasted `.scxml` file can contain a transition crossing `<parallel>` regions, and the validation pipeline won't flag it — only `onConnect`/`isValidConnection` guard the live gesture. (Cross-reference: `.claude/index.md`'s "Known issues" list.)
- **Typing nesting into the XML editor isn't prevented** — `normalizeParallelGroups` itself has no nesting guard, so 2+ Initial ids typed inside a region still produce a nested `<parallel>`; the validator reports it.
- **A region itself can't be selected in the diagram**, since regions are drawn as columns. Its own onentry/onexit, and transitions defined on the region element, have no node to edit them from — they must be edited in the XML.
- **A hand-written `<parallel>` with a single region is turned into a `<state>`**, since nothing distinguishes it from a converted one (accepted explicitly — `scxml.md` #12).
- **A member's `viz:xywh` doesn't round-trip through separation.** Once `separateParallelRegions` moves a member out of another region's span, that new x is what gets persisted; a manual placement overlapping another region is pulled back out on the next re-parse.
- **`isParallelGroupMember` is coarse-grained** — it disables `viz:xywh` priority for all flagged members, not just the case the original regression was about.
- **Legacy collapse is narrow**: `migrateLegacyStructure` only collapses an old `<state>` wrapper whose sole child is its marked `<parallel>`; an old wrapper that also held other `<state>` or `<final>` children keeps its nested shape (minus the markers).

## Important edge cases

- **Converting back is symmetric** — there's no "once parallel, always parallel" ratchet: deleting regions, or a hand-edit that leaves one region, turns the `<parallel>` back into a `<state>` on the next pass.
- **Every region, including a single-member one, is its own `*_region` state** (`scxml.md` #11) — a `<parallel>`'s direct children are never bare leaf states produced by this feature.
- **Regions can nest** — normalization is bottom-up, so a region whose own children grow a second Initial work tree is itself converted into a nested `<parallel>`.
- **Root `initial` holds the parallel's id**: `<scxml initial="__root_parallel">`. Marking a root sibling Initial writes the regions' ids plus the new one (`getInitialIds` expands the token), and the next pass adds it as a region and resets `initial` to `__root_parallel`.
- **Copy/paste of regions relies on normalization**: copied regions are recorded as Initial (`isParallelRegion` in the copy handler), `resolveCarriedOverInitialIds` writes them onto an empty `<state>` target's `initial`, and the next normalization pass converts that target into a `<parallel>`. A `<parallel>` target is skipped.
- **The divider-recompute race**: `onConnect`'s and the drag-stop position commit's SCXML updates hold `isUpdatingPositionRef` around their async re-parse; if the result lands inside that window, the resync effect drops it. `applyLatestEnhancedNodes` in `visual-diagram.tsx` is a safety-net retry both call sites invoke once they clear the gate.

## Things that must NOT be changed

- Do not read a wrapper node's `data.dividerLines` for anything that must be accurate **during** a drag or right after a connect/reparent — it's a parse-time snapshot. Use `computeLiveParallelDividerXs` (the exact bug class the divider-recompute race describes).
- Do not add a second, ad hoc cross-region-transition check — always call `wouldCrossParallelRegions` (`.claude/project/coding-rules.md` §3's "shared utility, never duplicated rules").
- Do not give `ParallelGroupWrapperNode` any visible border/background/label — column labels were tried and removed at the user's request.
- Do not treat `isRootParallel` as sufficient on its own — it must only apply to direct children of `<scxml>` (pass `'root'` as the container kind). A nested `<parallel>` sharing the id is ordinary (confirmed in PR review; regression tests in `parallel-group-normalization.test.ts` / `state-registry.test.ts`).
- Do not let regions take part in Initial-group analysis: they're always active, never Initial-designated (`groupAnalysisKind`, `isParallelRegion`).
- Do not move, copy or re-insert a state through `.state` only — use the tag-preserving helpers in `scxml-manipulation-utils.ts`, or a `<parallel>` silently becomes a `<state>`.
- Do not check a new id against the rendered diagram nodes alone — regions and `__root_parallel` are never rendered, so their ids would be reused. Use `collectExistingIds(scxmlDoc, nodes)` (`scxml-manipulation-utils.ts`), as paste and add-state do (found in PR review: copying a `<parallel>` duplicated its region ids).
